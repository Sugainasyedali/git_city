# Community conduct

Welcome contributors of every background and experience level. Critique decisions and code respectfully. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing and sharing confidential information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, restrict participation or suspend contributors to protect the community. Report an incident privately to a project maintainer. Share enough context to identify the incident and the outcome you seek. Maintainers should restrict disclosure to those handling the report and allow affected participants to respond.
